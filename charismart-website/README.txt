CHARISMART FOOD & LOGISTICS — ONE-PAGE WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: page content, product list, contact form and image references.
- style.css: theme, layout, mobile responsiveness and visual styles.
- script.js: mobile menu, product category filters and product enquiry selection.
- assets/charismart-logo.jpeg: supplied Charismart logo.

PREVIEW
Open index.html in a modern browser. Internet is required for the online sample photos and Google Fonts; the page layout itself is local.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Product and hero/about photos are sample online photos, not verified photos of Charismart's actual items. Replace each <img src="..."> URL in index.html with your real product photo path, for example assets/products/soursop-leaves.jpg. Replace the hero/about background image URLs in style.css as desired.
2. The contact form uses FormSubmit (https://formsubmit.co/) to forward enquiries to charismart809@gmail.com. On the first live submission, FormSubmit may send a verification email to that inbox; open it and approve the form before expecting enquiries to arrive. Test after publishing.
3. WhatsApp uses the international format +2349069252687, converted from the supplied Nigerian number 09069252687.
4. Update product names, availability and any shipping claims as the business requires. No prices have been invented.
5. Host all files together on your web host. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

EDIT PRODUCT PHOTOS
Find a product card in index.html and change its <img src="..."> to your own image. Example:
<img src="assets/products/soursop-dried-leaves.jpg" alt="Soursop dried leaves" loading="lazy">
Create the assets/products folder and add your photos there.
